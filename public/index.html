Usage limits

				
    
					
  • Request bodies can be up to 1 MiB. A larger body gets 413.
    • 
					
  • 
						Anything you can scale is capped rather than rejected: /delay at
						10 seconds, /bytes and /range at
						100,000 bytes, /stream at 100 lines,
						/drip at 100,000 bytes over 10 seconds,
						and redirect chains at 20 hops.
					
    • 
					
  • A malformed request gets a 4xx JSON response that says why.
    • 
					
  • 
						The service sets no rate limit of its own. Its operator can add one at the Cloudflare edge. If you are limited or blocked
						there, expect 429 Too Many Requests (wait for Retry-After when it is sent) or a challenge page, which
						API clients cannot solve.
					
    • 
					
  • 
						In front of the service, Cloudflare accepts URLs up to 16 KB and request headers up to 128 KB in total.
						Connection limits
					
    • 
				

			


			

				
Behind Cloudflare

				
    
					
  • 
						Headers. Requests pass through Cloudflare first, so /headers and every echo include what it
						adds, such as CF-Connecting-IP, CF-IPCountry, CF-Ray, CF-Visitor,
						X-Forwarded-For and X-Forwarded-Proto. It also sets Accept-Encoding to
						br, gzip whatever your client sent. Unlike httpbin.org, nothing is hidden by default: add
						?show_env=0 to see only what your client sent.
						Cloudflare HTTP headers
					
    • 
					
  • 
						Cookies. Cloudflare features on the zone can set their own cookies, for example __cf_bm or
						cf_clearance. Your client sends them back, so they appear in /cookies unless you add
						?show_env=0.
						Cloudflare cookies
					
    • 
					
  • 
						URLs. Cloudflare normalizes URLs before the service sees them. Repeated slashes are always merged, and
						depending on zone settings dot segments and percent-encoding are normalized too, so /anything can echo a
						slightly different path from the one you sent.
						URL normalization
					
    • 
					
  • 
						Errors. This service answers errors in JSON. Errors generated by Cloudflare itself (1xxx codes and its own
						5xx) are HTML unless your client asks for application/json or text/markdown in
						Accept.
						Error responses
					
    • 
					
  • 
						Trace. /cdn-cgi/trace is answered by Cloudflare, not by this service, and reports the data
						center, HTTP version and TLS version of your connection.
						The /cdn-cgi/ endpoint
